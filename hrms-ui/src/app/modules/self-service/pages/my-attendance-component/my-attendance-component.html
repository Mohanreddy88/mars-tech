How to fill your timesheet

  1. Working day: click the day and type your hours (0–24, e.g. 8 or 7.5).
  2. Leave: type the leave code instead of hours (e.g. AL). The box shows the code.
  3. Comment: with the day selected, click and write the reason. Required for every leave day.
  4. Leave request: if you didn't apply in My Leave, the leave is recorded for you when HR approves this timesheet. Add your MC letter to it under My Leave › My Requests.
  5. Save any time; tick the agreement and Submit when the period is complete.
