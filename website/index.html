AI assistant

Ask in plain words. Get answers from your own records.

The built-in help assistant answers common questions for free. Connect AI and it also answers personal questions — always limited to what that login is allowed to see.

  • Parents: "How much do I owe?", "Did Aisha come to school today?"
  • Staff: "What's my leave balance?", "Download my last payslip."
  • Admins: "Any unpaid invoices?" — with a button to the page and an Excel download.
  • Replies in the language people write in — English, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese and more.
  • Read-only and private: it can't change anything or see other people's data.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can we use Mars HRMS in our country?

Yes — HR, attendance, leave, the school and parent features, the mobile apps and the assistant work for companies and schools anywhere, with your own public holidays and in the language your people use. Payroll statutory deductions currently follow Malaysian rules (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB); talk to us about payroll for your country.

Can we use it for HR only, or for our school only?

Both work. HR and payroll are always included; the school features (classes, parents, daily reports, fees, messages) are switched on per company.

Do parents and staff need to install anything?

No — everything works in the browser. For the best experience they can install the Mars HRMS app for Android or iPhone and get push notifications.

How do we sign in?

Each company has its own address, e.g. yourschool.marshrms.com. On this website click Sign in and enter your company code; in the mobile app you enter the same code once.

Is the AI assistant required?

No. The help assistant answers your own FAQ list for free. AI answers are optional: an admin connects your Anthropic (Claude) account and can switch them off at any time.

Can we bring in our existing employee and student data?

Talk to us during the demo about your current data — employees, classes, children and parents — and how to set it up in Mars HRMS.
